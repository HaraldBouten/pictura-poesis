Archive · 2012—Present

The
History.

A decade of music, art, stages and changing chapters.

The beginning

Built around
an idea.

From its earliest recordings, Pictura Poesis approached visual art and literature not as decoration, but as material to question, interpret and transform.

The band's musical language evolved across the years, but the foundation remained recognizable: orchestral colour, extreme metal and subjects drawn from art, mythology, history and literature.

Timeline

Chapters.

2012—13

The first chapter

In Death's Embrace

The earliest material was written during the band's formative period and became the first Pictura Poesis demo in 2013.

The recording introduced the relationship between extreme metal, orchestration and subjects taken from visual art.

Demo · 2013
2014

Expanding the sound

Demonic Delusions

A five-track EP followed, recorded at Tombstone Studio in Nieuwstadt.

The release was followed by shows including support appearances with Ancient Rites, Rotting Christ, Herfst and Madgod.

EP · 2014
2015—16

Towards the first full length

Writing a larger world.

Work began on a full-length record while the band continued performing and developing its orchestral extreme-metal sound.

Shows during this period included appearances alongside acts such as Dood, Matricidium and Slechtvalk.

2017

First full length

Miseriae Mundi

Ten tracks built around war, mythology, history, mortality and human suffering became the band's first full-length record.

The release was followed by festival appearances including Beukfest, VDF2 and Brabant Metalfest.

Full Length · 2017
2018

Revisiting the archive

Nightmares

Nightmares revisited material from the band's earlier years in a new production and orchestral context.

The following live period expanded outside the Netherlands, with performances in Germany, Belgium, Slovenia and Iceland.

Full Length · 2018
2019

Beyond the Netherlands

International stages.

The touring chapter continued into 2019, including an appearance at MetalDays in Slovenia alongside further club and festival performances.

2022

Reinterpretation

Death and the Maiden

One of the earliest Pictura Poesis compositions returned in a completely newly recorded version.

The single connected the early catalogue with the band's later musical language.

Single · 2022
2026—27

The next chapter

A new work takes shape.

A ten-track record is currently being completed, rebuilding the sound of Pictura Poesis around its present-day musical identity.

Instruments have been recorded. Vocals, artwork, mixing and mastering form the final stages of the new chapter.
