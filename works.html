About the collection

Every song begins somewhere outside music.

A painting, an image or a story preserved in pigment becomes the first note. This collection presents each song once, with every released version gathered inside its detail view. All reproduced images are public domain or openly licensed. Where a modern source work remains protected, the exhibition leaves the image absent instead of replacing it.
