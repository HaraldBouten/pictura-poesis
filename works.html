The Gallery

The
Works.

The image is not decoration. It is where the music begins.

Ut pictura poesis

From image
to sound.

Paintings, literature, history and mythology are used as starting points rather than scenery.

A subject is isolated from its original work, examined critically and placed in a wider historical or social context before being translated into lyrics and music.

The music then amplifies the atmosphere of that subject — orchestration, extreme metal and individual musical voices combining into a world around the original image or story.

I

Source

Observe.

A painting, poem, myth, person or historical event creates the starting point.

II

Context

Question.

The work is separated from the obvious reading and examined from different perspectives.

III

Translation

Transform.

Lyrics, orchestration and extreme metal create a new musical environment around the subject.
