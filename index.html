Art & Music

Every work
has a story.

The image is not decoration. It is where the music begins.

Pictura Poesis draws inspiration from paintings, poems, mythology and historical events. A subject is examined, questioned and transformed into a musical world of its own.

The Band

Pictura
Poesis

Symphonic extreme metal from the south of the Netherlands.

Pictura Poesis combines orchestral grandeur with elements of death and black metal, using visual art, literature, history and mythology as the starting point for its music.

Rather than treating a painting or historical event as simple subject matter, the band aims to explore its context, atmosphere and darker implications.
