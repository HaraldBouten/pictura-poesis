Art & Music

Every work
has a story.

The image is not decoration. It is where the music begins.

Paintings, poems, mythology and historical events form the starting point for Pictura Poesis. Their stories are examined, questioned and transformed into musical worlds of their own.

The Band

Pictura
Poesis

Symphonic extreme metal from the south of the Netherlands.

Pictura Poesis combines orchestral grandeur with elements of death and black metal, using visual art, literature, history and mythology as the foundation for its music.

Rather than treating a painting or historical event as simple subject matter, the band explores its context, atmosphere and darker implications.
